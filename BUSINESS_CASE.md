# Business Case

## Superstore Retail Sales Analysis

You are working as a Junior Business Analyst for a retail company.

Management has sales data but needs a simple and clear view of business performance.

They want to understand:
- How sales change over time
- Which categories and products perform best
- Which customers contribute most to sales
- Which regions perform well
- How shipping modes and delivery times compare

Your role is to analyze the data and turn it into useful business information.

**Excel → SQL → Statistics → Power BI → AI → Business Recommendations**

The final result should help management understand the business and decide where further attention is needed.
